### About

I'm Chris, a full-stack dev and security researcher from Denmark. Nowadays I mostly write fast backends in Rust and Node.js, but I'm happy across the whole stack and move between projects a lot, frontends, full apps, tooling, whatever the thing actually needs. A few years in security research taught me to build stuff that's hard to break and easy to trust.

Outside of that I'm heavy into crypto, Bitcoin, Monero, Litecoin, Solana, the DeFi side and the backend systems behind it, and I'm always pulling on machine learning and LLMs, hosted and local. I run Fedora and pretty much live in the terminal.

I care more about what I'm building than the title on it, something useful, profitable, or made for a good reason. If that's what you're working on, I'd genuinely love to help. Open to roles, freelance, and collaborations.

More of my work over at [chrisch88.dev](https://chrisch88.dev).
